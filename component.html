I'm a last-year student in Software Engineering bachelor's degree at Metropolia UAS, where I'm learning to build for the web and mobiles. Below is a bit about how I got here, what I'm working on, and how I think about the tools I'm learning to use.

How I got into this

Back in 2015, I earned a bachelor's degree in Management and after that I entered the world of IT, working for three years as an Electronic Data Interchange (EDI) specialist in a major IT company that provides a vast variety of EDI and SaaS solutions for businesses. I gained a deep understanding of B2B EDI and supply chain, developed skills in efficiently organizing IT workflows in teams, and improved my ability to communicate with clients and partners.

During this time, I realized I wanted to learn more about technology and pursue software development, which became my true passion.

What I'm learning

I completed a vocational degree in ICT at Keuda Vocational School in Kerava in 2022, studying entirely in Finnish. I worked as a frontend developer for 1 year and got solid skills on web development using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React and TypeScript.

I am in the final year of a bachelor's degree in Software Engineering at Metropolia AMK in Helsinki. I have learned Java, JavaScript, Kotlin and Python, databases, React, Node.js, Spring Boot and other essential software development subjects. Currently, I am working as a Software Engineering Intern at Zalando, where I am contributing to frontend work on a real product team while learning how large codebases, code reviews, and collaboration work in practice.
